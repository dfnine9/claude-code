# mixie — Product Concept Brief

*Strawberry social tonic · THC-free / kava-free · clear-glass 2 oz shot*

> Working document compiled from a concept brainstorm. All doses, costs, and
> formulation notes are **conceptual, for R&D discussion only** — every number
> needs bench validation and regulatory/legal review before it means anything.

---

## 1. The one-line concept

A pink, clear-glass **2 oz social tonic shot** that delivers a Brez-style
feeling — calm, present, gently mood-lifted, sociable, no intoxication — but
hits faster, adds a saffron mood-lift Brez doesn't have, and stays inside the
clean-label lane that keeps it on a **mainstream shelf (Target)** instead of
behind the smoke-shop counter. Sip it straight, or pour it into any drink.

**Brand name:** *mixie* (working)
**Flavor / color:** Strawberry, strawberry-pink
**Format:** 2 oz (60 mL) clear flint glass shot bottle
**Positioning:** Social / cocktail-alternative — not "focus," not "sleep," not "buzz-with-baggage"

---

## 2. How we got here — the format exploration

The original question was about **form factors for a social tonic that drops
into any drink** — ideas that hadn't been done. The full idea set:

**Dissolving / melting**
- Functional ice cubes/spheres (actives frozen in; drink "builds" as it melts — ramping onset story)
- Oral-film-style dissolving leaf that floats and shimmers on the surface
- Ritual effervescent cube (modern absinthe-sugar-cube homage)
- Dissolvable stirrer — the swizzle stick *is* the dose (self-portioning)

**Structural / garnish**
- Bursting pearls (boba/caviar-style) that pop and release
- Active-infused garnish (dehydrated citrus wheel / hibiscus flower carrying the dose)
- Rimming salt/sugar with actives (micro-dose sip by sip)
- Release straw (blend coated inside)

**Liquid-adjacent**
- Atomizer mist over the drink (aroma-forward, shareable ritual)
- Single-squeeze liquid pods (Mio-style)
- **2 oz shot** ← where we landed

Early bets were the **functional ice cube** and **dissolvable stirrer** for
novelty. We converged on the **shot** because it's the only format with no
payload constraint and the most proven retail economics.

---

## 3. The payload problem (why format matters)

Target payload = a **full THC-free Brez Flow serving ≈ 400 mg of actives**.

Brez Flow's actual deck (from the can): lion's mane extract (marketed "2,200 mg"),
cacao extract, L-theanine, black seed oil, in a base of carbonated water, agave,
lemon, elderflower, chicory inulin.

**Honest payload breakdown (~400 mg):**

| Ingredient | Realistic mass | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Lion's mane 10:1 extract | ~150 mg | "2,200 mg" is fresh-weight; ~90% water. 10:1 extract makes an honest equivalence at ~150 mg |
| L-theanine | 100 mg | Pure amino acid — **cannot be concentrated**, the immovable mass |
| Cacao / theobromine | ~100 mg | Concentrate to high-theobromine extract; bitterness reads as intentional |
| Black seed oil | ~50 mg | Oil — needs microencapsulation / emulsification |

**Key insight:** the scary number on the can (2,200 mg) is mostly water weight
you don't have to carry. The only thing you truly can't shrink is the theanine.

### Why a Listerine-style strip fails
A breath-strip-size film (~50 mg total, ~30–50% active max) carries only
**~20 mg** of actives — about **5%** of the dose. Strips are a microdose or
"theatrical ignition" format, not a full-dose one.

## 4. Form-factor size comparison (true-scale)

Payload held constant at ~400 mg actives. Can = benchmark.

| Format | Footprint | Thickness | Total mass | Actives | % of dose | Dissolves cold? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Brez Flow can (benchmark) | ⌀58 × 157 mm | — | 372 g | ≈400 mg | 100% | Is the drink |
| **Pink 2 oz shot** | ⌀45 × 75 mm | — | ~130 g | 400 mg (room for ~1.5 g) | 100% | Pours into anything / drink straight |
| Listerine-size strip | 32 × 22 mm | 0.06 mm | ~50 mg | ~20 mg | ≈5% | Yes, instantly |
| Single leaf wafer | 38 × 28 mm | 0.5 mm | ~650 mg | 400 mg | 100% | Yes, ~60–90 s |
| Two-leaf serve | 28 × 20 mm ×2 | 0.5 mm ea | ~325 mg ea | 200 mg ea | 100% | Yes, faster |
| Fast-dissolve mini-tab | ⌀13 mm | 4 mm | ~600 mg | 400 mg | 100% | Yes, with fizz |
| Gummy | 20 × 20 mm | 8 mm | ~4 g | 400 mg | 100% | **No** — chew or rim-garnish |

> Interactive visual: **Form-factor scale study** — https://claude.ai/code/artifact/db987095-1174-42ce-a5ab-84d75c53ec17

**Takeaways**
- Strip = ~5% of the dose. Only good as a microdose/theatrical add-on.
- Leaf wafer & mini-tab = full dose *and* keep the "transforms any drink" magic.
- Gummy = easiest to manufacture, but doesn't dissolve cold → chew or rim garnish only.
- **Shot = no payload constraint at all** (60 mL dissolves the dose with ~3× headroom).

---

## 5. Why the shot won

The shot is the format where the **dose problem disappears** — the constraints
shift from *mass* to *chemistry* and *taste*.

**Advantages**
- Proven economics: 5-hour Energy built the category; Feel Free proved $8+ at gas stations.
- Fast onset possible (nano-emulsification → 5–10 min vs. the can's ~20 min sipped).
- Clear glass = a positioning statement ("nothing to hide") vs. Feel Free's opaque cobalt.
- Feel Free's ritual mechanic comes free: "drink half, feel it, finish it" = light/full dosing.

**Trade-off**
- The shot is its own drink (or a pour-in), not a per-drink *transformation moment*.
  A dissolvable (leaf/tab) would own the "turns any drink into the tonic" story.
- Reasonable play: **shot as the volume hero, a dissolvable as the differentiator SKU.**

**Chemistry cautions for the clear pink shot**
- Black seed oil needs an emulsifier (quillaja / gum acacia) or it rings the neck.
- Lion's mane + cacao extracts run brown → use **decolorized/clarified** extracts.
- Keep theobromine **under ~0.3 g/L** or it recrystallizes (cold-crash sediment).
- Clear flint glass invites UV fade of pigment + actives → spec UV-inhibited glass or accept shorter shelf life.

## 7. Differentiation strategy

Keep Brez's base intact (that *is* the familiar feeling), then hang the brand
on **one signature molecule** Brez doesn't use, plus non-ingredient levers.

**Keep (the Brez feeling):** lion's mane + L-theanine + a cacao/theobromine mood note.

**Signature "glow" molecule — options:**
- **Saffron (affron / Safr'Inside)** ← *chosen.* Clinically validated mood-lift (~28 mg),
  premium story, leans pink-gold, Target-safe. The smart pick.
- **Kanna (Zembrin / Sceletium tortuosum)** — stronger *social* effect, but serotonergic
  (needs SSRI/MAOI warning) and grayer food-ingredient status. Reserve for a separate
  "night out / elevate" SKU, not the Target hero.
- **Isolated theobromine** — cleaner warm glow than cacao extract; also drops the brown color.

**Supporting cast (pick 1–2, don't kitchen-sink):**
- Citicoline (Cognizin) — clarity / "present" feeling (Magic Mind uses it)
- Magnesium glycinate/L-threonate — the "shoulders drop" unclench
- Ashwagandha (KSM-66) — cortisol/unclench (overlaps theanine + mag)
- L-citrulline — novel warm-*flush* mimicking a first drink (needs ~1 g+ to actually feel)

**Non-ingredient differentiators (may matter most):**
- **Fast nano-onset** (5–10 min) — makes it feel like a drink, not a supplement. Ownable.
- **Bitter/aromatic drink-signal** (gentian/orange bitters + citrus oil) — cues "adult drink," pure sensory theater.

## 8. Final formulation spec (the focused stack)

Everything that carries the feeling + the differentiators, nothing that's dead weight.

| Ingredient | Dose | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Lion's mane (clarified) | 150 mg | Base — cognitive/focus |
| L-theanine | 100 mg | Base — calm focus |
| Saffron (affron) | 28 mg | **Signature** mood-lift |
| Citicoline (Cognizin) | 250 mg | Clarity / present |
| Theobromine (isolated) | 25 mg | Warm cacao glow (stays in solution) |
| Magnesium glycinate | ~350 mg | Non-laxative unclench |
| Black seed oil (nano-emulsified) | 50 mg | Brez fidelity + fast onset |
| **Total actives** | **~1.3 g** | Dissolves clean, stays pink, tastes manageable |

**Deliberately cut:**
- **L-citrulline** — its warm-flush needs a full gram to be felt; too dose-hungry to fit honestly here.
- **Ashwagandha** — overlaps theanine/magnesium's job while adding the most cloud + bitterness per mg.
- Both belong in a larger canned format where mass + masking aren't fighting each other.

### The "everything" version (for reference — why we didn't)
A full 9-active stack totals **~2.7 g** and *physically* fits in 60 mL (Magic Mind /
Feel Free carry similar loads). But three walls force compromises:
1. **Theobromine cold-crashes** at 100 mg/60 mL (~5× cold solubility) → sediment.
2. **Magnesium & citrulline are bulk/GI-limited** → end up under-dosed vs. standalone efficacy.
3. **Cumulative bitterness** is the real ceiling — 60 mL can't mask 6+ bitter actives; clarity/pink also suffers.
   Plus the **strategic** wall: 9 actives = each below threshold + a kitchen-sink label = "does this even work?" skepticism.

**Focused deck feels stronger because each ingredient is dosed to actually do its job.**

## 9. Flavor & color — Strawberry

Strawberry does three jobs at once:

1. **Earns the pink** — a strawberry shot is *supposed* to be pink; color stops being a burden.
2. **Best bitterness mask** — strawberry's high-impact esters + acid + sweet cover earthy/bitter
   actives more aggressively than the delicate lemon-elderflower profile could.
3. **Makes signatures taste intentional** — strawberry + saffron is a fine-dining pairing (honeyed,
   "expensive"); strawberry + theobromine = chocolate-covered strawberry; strawberry + black-pepper
   note of black seed oil reads as a designed detail.

**Color chemistry gotcha:** strawberry's own red (pelargonidin anthocyanins) fades to dull
orange-brown under light/time — won't hold in clear glass. **Fix:** flavor with natural
strawberry, but borrow color *stability* from **acylated anthocyanins (black carrot / red radish)** —
the standard stable natural red for clear-bottle beverages. Add UV-inhibited glass or accept fade.

**Positioning shift:** strawberry leans warmer, friendlier, more crowd-pleasing than
lemon-elderflower's sophisticated-adaptogen angle — and carries frozen-cocktail associations
(daiquiri, margarita) that reinforce the *social / cocktail-alternative* occasion. Net win for
mainstream retail. Possible line: **Strawberry = flagship; blood orange or hibiscus-lime = SKU 2.**

## 10. Naming

Chosen (working): **mixie** — short, warm, playful (Poppi / Hiyo / Cann lane), nods to
"mixes into anything."

> **Clearance caveat:** "Mixie" is a common term for a blender/mixer-grinder in South Asian
> markets with existing uses → may be hard to trademark. Spellings **Mixi / Mixly** dodge some
> of it. Run a real availability scan before committing.

**Alternatives explored (by angle):**

- *Mixes-into-anything:* Mixie, Mixi, Mixly, Swirl/Swirlie, Dash, Splash
- *The feeling:* **Hum** (sleeper favorite — "gives you a little hum"), Glimmer, Glow, Sway, Buzzy, Mellow
- *Strawberry-pink:* **Blush** (pink + the engineered warm-flush + social/flirty = triple meaning), Rosie, Punch, Coral, Nectar
- *Social ritual:* Clink, Round ("next round's on me"), Cheers, Sesh

**Top 3 ranked by how hard the name works:**
1. **Blush** — pink + names the warm-flush effect + social ease. (Clearance risk: common word.)
2. **Hum** — coolest, most ownable, self-writing tagline.
3. **Mixie / Mixi** — the chosen instinct; needs the trademark check.

- [ ] **Bench the focused formula** — solubility, cold-crash, clarity, taste-masking in 60 mL.
