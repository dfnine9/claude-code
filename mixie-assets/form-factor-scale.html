Assumptions behind the numbers

  • The 400 mg payload: lion's mane 10:1 dual extract ~150 mg (an honest equivalence to the can's "2,200 mg" fresh-weight claim), L-theanine 100 mg (pure amino acid — cannot be concentrated), high-theobromine cacao extract ~100 mg, spray-dried black seed oil ~50 mg.
  • Pink shot: 60 mL of water dissolves the whole payload with roughly 3× headroom — the constraints shift from mass to chemistry. Black seed oil needs an emulsifier (quillaja or gum acacia) or it rings the neck; lion's mane and cacao extracts run brown, so a true pink needs decolorized extracts plus an acid-stable natural pink (hibiscus or dragon-fruit pigments hold at pH < 4, which the lemon profile wants anyway); keep theobromine under ~0.3 g/L or it recrystallizes when chilled. Clear flint glass invites UV fading of both pigment and actives — spec a UV-inhibited flint or accept a shorter shelf life than amber/cobalt would give.
  • Strip: film payloads cap near 30–50% of total mass; a breath-strip-size film tops out around 20 mg of actives, hence ≈5% of the dose.
  • Leaf wafer: ~60% active loading in a 0.5 mm pullulan/HPMC film at ~1.2 g/cm³. Two smaller leaves cast with tighter dose uniformity than one large one.
  • Mini-tab: compressed fast-dissolve format. A fully effervescent version (citric + bicarbonate couple) grows to roughly ⌀16 × 4.5 mm — still smaller than the quarter.
  • Gummy: supplement gummies run ~10% active loading, so 400 mg of actives needs a ~4 g piece — one large square gummy, or two standard bears. Gelatin/pectin does not dissolve in a cold drink; it pairs with one, or rides the rim as a garnish.
